Prologue: The Time Traveler's Code

In the year 3024, humanity discovered the secrets of time travel. With great power came even greater danger. The Time Machine, once a marvel of technology, has shattered into fragments across various epochs. These fragments, holding the Code of Eternity, must be recovered to restore balance to the timeline.

As an agent of the Temporal Defense Force, your mission is clear: retrieve these fragments before they fall into the wrong hands. Each fragment is guarded by challenges and riddles that only the sharpest minds can solve.

Your journey begins now. Are you ready to traverse the annals of history and piece together The Code of Eternity?
